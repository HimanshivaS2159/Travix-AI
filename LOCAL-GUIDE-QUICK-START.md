# Local Guide Agent - Quick Start Guide 🗺️

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Minutes

The Local Guide Agent helps you discover the best attractions, restaurants, travel tips, and hidden gems in your destination cities.

## 📍 Supported Cities

Currently available:
- 🇮🇳 **Delhi** (India)
- 🇮🇳 **Mumbai** (India)
- 🇦🇪 **Dubai** (UAE)
- 🇮🇳 **Bangalore** (India)

## 💬 How to Use

### Simple Queries

Just type naturally in the chat:

```
"Show me attractions in Delhi"
"Where should I eat in Mumbai?"
"Give me travel tips for Dubai"
"Hidden gems in Bangalore"
"Complete guide for Delhi"
```

### What You'll Get

#### 1️⃣ Attractions
- **Name & Rating** (e.g., Red Fort - 4.6⭐)
- **Entry Fees** (Indian & Foreign prices)
- **Visit Duration** (e.g., 2-3 hours)
- **Best Time** to visit
- **Insider Tips** (avoid crowds, what to see)

#### 2️⃣ Restaurants
- **Cuisine Type** (Mughlai, South Indian, etc.)
- **Price Range** (₹, ₹₹, ₹₹₹, ₹₹₹₹)
- **Must-Try Dishes** (local specialties)
- **Ratings** from locals

#### 3️⃣ Travel Tips
- 🚇 **Transportation** (metro, taxi, etc.)
- 🛡️ **Safety** guidelines
- ☀️ **Weather** recommendations
- 🍽️ **Food** suggestions
- 💰 **Money** tips
- 🛍️ **Shopping** advice

#### 4️⃣ Hidden Gems
- Secret spots locals love
- Off-the-beaten-path experiences
- Unique places tourists miss

## 🎯 Example Conversations

### Example 1: Planning Delhi Trip
**You:** "I'm going to Delhi next week. What should I see?"

**Local Guide Agent:**
```

📍 Top Attractions:
1. Red Fort (4.6⭐) - ₹50

   - Light & sound show in evening

2. Qutub Minar (4.5⭐) - ₹40
   - Great for photography
   - Explore Iron Pillar

3. India Gate (4.7⭐) - Free
   - Evening walk perfect
   - Beautiful when lit at night

🍽️ Where to Eat:
1. Karim's - Authentic Mughlai
   - Must try: Mutton Korma

2. Paranthe Wali Gali - Street Food
   - Try: Stuffed Parathas

💡 Essential Tips:
- Use Delhi Metro - clean & efficient
- October to March is best time
- Dress modestly at religious sites
```
